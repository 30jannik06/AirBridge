**AirBridge 0.3.0 plays your music.** Pick AirBridge in the AirPlay menu of any iPhone app and get sound plus a "now playing"
view with cover, title and a live spectrum. Screen mirroring works as before.

## What's new

- 🎵 **Music over AirPlay** (ALAC): sound with very low delay, volume follows the iPhone buttons
- 🖼️ **"Now playing" view:** cover art, title, artist, album, progress bar with time and a live spectrum
- ⏯️ **Remote control:** play/pause, next and previous right from the PC, where the iPhone allows it
- 🔒 **"Trusted devices only" mode:** unknown devices are rejected silently, for unattended use
- ℹ️ The status tab now also shows music connections and the current track

## Please note

- **Music has sound in these downloads. The sound that goes with screen mirroring does not.** That one needs the
  AAC-ELD codec (FDK-AAC), whose licence is not compatible with the GPL, so it cannot be shipped in a binary. You can build
  it yourself with `--features nonfree-audio`
  (see [docs/BUILDING.md](https://github.com/30jannik06/AirBridge/blob/main/docs/BUILDING.md)).
- The remote-control buttons depend on your iPhone allowing it. If they stay grey, control the music on the phone.
- The "allow this device?" prompt and the "trusted devices only" mode are access checks, not a PIN.
- Windows only, one iPhone at a time, no DRM content (Netflix, Disney+, …).
- Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the files are not code-signed yet.

Updating from 0.2.x is safe: key, name and settings carry over, and the iPhone still recognises your receiver.

## 🇩🇪 Kurzfassung

**AirBridge 0.3.0 spielt Musik.** Im AirPlay-Menü einer beliebigen iPhone-App „AirBridge“ wählen: Ton plus „Läuft gerade“-Ansicht
mit Cover, Titel und Live-Spektrum.

- Musik über AirPlay (ALAC), die Lautstärketasten am iPhone steuern die Lautstärke.
- Play/Pause/Weiter/Zurück vom PC aus, sofern das iPhone es erlaubt.
- Neuer Modus „Nur vertraute Geräte“. Das ist keine PIN.
- **Musik hat in den Downloads Ton, der Ton beim Bildschirmspiegeln nicht** (FDK-AAC ist nicht GPL-kompatibel). Dafür selbst
  bauen mit `--features nonfree-audio`.
- **Setup** oder **portable ZIP**, Prüfsummen in `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Windows kann eine SmartScreen-Warnung zeigen.
